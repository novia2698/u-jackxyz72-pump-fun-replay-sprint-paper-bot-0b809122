What to build: a TypeScript paper-trading bot (main.ts at the repo root,
run by the grader as node main.ts) that trades 10 SOL of paper money on a
24-hour replay of real pump.fun bonding-curve events. The harness streams
hello / batch / done messages; the bot answers each batch with its orders
for that slot. Ranking is by net PnL, and only a net PnL above zero can take
a paid place. The bar to clear: a round trip of at least 0.01 SOL on at
least 20 different coins, and a maximum drawdown of 50% or less.

The bot built here is a momentum sniper with a pyramid: it only buys a coin
once real buyers have shown up (several trades, at least 1 SOL of real
reserve, curve price up 10% from creation — or 3 SOL of reserve on trade
flow alone — and all 10 of the coin's last 10 trades buys), and the
first buy is only a 0.02 SOL probe. A coin that keeps
running earns bigger adds (0.05 SOL when its real reserve grows 1.5x and its
price is up 10% from the probe, 0.08 SOL at 3x reserve and +20%); a coin
that stalls never gets a second lamport. It holds at most 5 positions, and
exits on a ladder — sell everything at +25% (banking the pop in full; the
half left riding in an earlier version gave most of its gain back), a
trailing stop once +25% was seen, a hard stop-loss at −12%, and a
stale-coin exit after ~3,600 slots. It only buys a coin still trading near its own highs, never
one that already faded off its peak. All decisions are deterministic
functions of the replayed batches (no clock, no network, no unseeded
randomness), so two runs on the same window give the same order log.
